ABOUT / PRIVACY POLICY

Your information. Handled with care.

How Smart Power website enquiries and company workspace records are handled.

Information we collect

Contact forms collect your name, phone number, project type and message. Company accounts store your name, email and a password hash. Optional profile details include contact information, NIC, date of birth, photographs, signature images, employment information, emergency contacts and bank details.

Company workspace records

Attendance timestamps, assigned tasks, work updates, site photos, support requests and payroll records are stored for company operations. A signature image is a saved image, not a cryptographic signing certificate.

How information is used

Information supports enquiry follow-up, account access, project coordination, staff administration, payroll records and office support. The website does not process online card payments.

Who can access records

Public pages are separate from protected company workspaces. Staff can access their own account-linked records. Admin accounts can manage company records and authorised staff information.

Cookies and browser storage

Sign-in uses a server-side session with an HttpOnly cookie, normally valid for 12 hours. Your chosen website language is remembered in browser local storage. You can clear this through your browser settings.

Storage and retention

Records are stored in the company application database. Storage and backups depend on the environment used to run the application. Contact the office about retention, correction or removal of a record; operational recordkeeping needs may affect the request.

Your choices and contact

You can update your profile through your signed-in workspace and contact the office about personal information or this policy. Call 077 358 7109 or visit 2/3, Sinna Aalim Road, Nintavur-15, 32340.

Updates to this page

This notice describes the current website and company application. It should be reviewed when hosting, integrations or information-handling practices change.
