INSTALLATION

Install on your Android phone

  1. Tap Download Android APK and wait for the file to finish downloading.
  2. Open the downloaded APK. Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser.
  3. Review the app name and icon, then tap Install.
  4. Open Smart Power and choose Client, Staff, Admin or Guest access.
Important

Android may show an “unknown source” notice because this app is installed outside Google Play. Do not download copies shared from unrelated websites or messaging groups.
